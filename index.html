    How it works

    Pick the day and an item, press Start mic and say it. Your pitch line starts when your voice does, and the take ends after a second of silence. The shaded bands are the plan's targets, 5 Hz either side; the faint line is your previous take. After each take your line is stretched to the length of the target, so the score compares shape and height, not speed. Slower and Slow stretch the targets while you speak.

    Save this take keeps the recording and its pitch line on this device, under the item you said it for. Tap Play on a saved take to hear it again while a marker runs along your line, so you can hear where it left the band. Saved takes stay until you delete them; they don't sync between your iPhone and Mac. Download saves a take as a WAV file (through the share sheet on iPhone: pick Save to Files), named like your Praat files, so you can keep it in iCloud Drive and open it in Praat.

    Where the microphone isn't allowed (inside claude.ai), record the item in Voice Memos, share it to Files, and use Load a recording. The page measures the whole recording and draws it the same way.
